# OOS and Walk-Forward Selection Design

## Goal

Make out-of-sample performance and walk-forward stability mandatory factor-admission checks rather than report-only diagnostics.

## Scope

The existing IS checks for Rank IC, ICIR, coverage, missingness, drawdown, and IC decay remain in place. This change adds three default admission checks:

1. The OOS mean Rank IC must have the same non-zero direction as the IS mean Rank IC.
2. The absolute OOS mean Rank IC must be at least 0.01.
3. Walk-forward analysis must have data, at least 60 percent positive windows, and consistent IC direction across all windows.

Factors that fail any check remain visible in the response, report, and diagnostics, but are excluded from `selected_factors` and therefore from multi-factor combinations.

## Design

`FactorScore` will carry the walk-forward positive-window ratio, sign-consistency result, and insufficient-data indicator already computed in `RunBacktestNode`.

`FactorSelector` will accept threshold parameters for the OOS magnitude and walk-forward positive ratio. Its shared selection and rejection-reason paths will apply identical rules, preventing a factor from being rejected without an explanation.

`SelectFactorsNode` will map the metrics generated by `RunBacktestNode` into the extended score. No request or response field is removed. Existing reports continue to render all diagnostics; their selected-factor section naturally reflects the stricter decision.

## Error Handling

Missing or insufficient OOS and walk-forward data is conservative: the factor is rejected with a specific reason. The workflow continues and produces a report rather than failing the complete research run.

## Tests

Selector tests will cover a factor that passes all gates, negative OOS direction, sub-threshold OOS magnitude, insufficient walk-forward data, inconsistent walk-forward signs, and an inadequate positive-window ratio. Graph/API coverage will verify that metrics reach the selector and rejected factors do not enter the combination result.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. A factor with negative or too-small OOS Rank IC cannot be selected.
2. A factor with insufficient, inconsistent, or less than 60 percent positive walk-forward windows cannot be selected.
3. Rejection output names the failed OOS or walk-forward criterion.
4. Existing response fields remain backward compatible and the full test suite passes.
